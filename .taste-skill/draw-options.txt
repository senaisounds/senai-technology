# 3 directions dealt for this brief

Choose the one whose way of composing can best carry an idea from your inventory (SKILL.md steps 1 and 4), not the
most familiar one. Then deal it in full with the same command plus `--direction <id>` (and without --options).

## `dense-tiles` — The well-kept board
Idea engine: The whole page is a board of tiles fitted edge to edge with narrow gaps, like a tidy desk seen from above. Each tile is one thing: the name, a statement, a photograph, a number, a link, a short list.
Composition: A 12-column grid with 8px gaps and 8–12px outer margins, filled completely, no empty cells. Tiles use only four sizes (for example 6×4, 6×2, 3×2, 3×4 in column × row units of about 90px).

## `contact-sheet` — The contact sheet
Idea engine: Show everything at once, small, in order. The work is laid out like a photographer's contact sheet or an archive drawer: dozens of small frames in strict rows, with all text set at one small size.
Composition: The name, or one sentence about the work, sits at display size (6–9vw) across the top of the first screen, and the sheet begins directly under it, still inside the first screen. The first screen opens with three or four short blocks of small text across the top on a 12-column grid (who, what, where to reach them), all at 14–16px, and immediately below, the sheet begins: rows of thumbnails at a fixed height (90–140px) with their natural widths, 6–10px gaps, each project forming a run of four to eight frames preceded by its title in the same small text.
Needs: many pictures. Without photographs of its own, each is a real picture of the subject's world found with scripts/assets.mjs, or a drawing you make.

## `name-as-monument` — The name as a monument
Idea engine: The name is the headline. Set it so large that it becomes architecture: as wide as the viewport, cropped by an edge, or stacked to fill the screen.
Composition: First screen: the wordmark spans 88–100% of the viewport width (font-size 16–24vw, set with a clamp so it never wraps), anchored either to the bottom edge with its descenders nearly touching it, or dead centre with everything else pushed to the corners. One line of supporting text at 16–20px sits in a corner on a shared margin; navigation is three or four words in the opposite corner.
