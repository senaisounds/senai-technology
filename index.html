Questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted after a short brief, because scope varies a lot. The three tiers tell you what each level includes; we reply to every enquiry within two business days.

How do payments work?

Clients in the United States pay by card or invoice through Stripe. Clients in Ethiopia pay locally in birr through Chapa.

What does “AI-built” mean?

We use AI-accelerated workflows for drafts, code and imagery, which makes us faster. Every detail is then reviewed and finished by hand: motion, responsiveness, performance and accessibility.

How long does it take?

It depends on the tier and your content. Launch is the quickest; you get a timeline together with the quote.

Can I update the site myself?

Studio and Signature include a simple way to update content, and every site ends with a walkthrough so you know how it all fits together.
